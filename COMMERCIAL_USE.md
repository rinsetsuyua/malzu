# Commercial Use

Status: public commercial-use policy.

Malzu is intended to stay free for non-commercial community research.

Commercial use is out of scope.

## Allowed Without Commercial Permission

- individual learning
- hobby research
- independent non-commercial analysis
- unpaid community research
- classroom or academic use that is not commercialized
- public forks under compatible non-commercial terms
- reviewed community contributions

## Not Allowed

- commercial threat-intelligence products
- paid SOC tooling
- vendor enrichment
- paid consulting deliverables
- SaaS wrapping
- private commercial forks
- resale of the dataset, UI, or derived service
- bundling into commercial malware-analysis platforms
- use as a commercial lead-generation or enrichment dataset

## Commercial License

No commercial license is offered by default.

If that changes later, it should require a separate explicit policy decision.

## Naming

Describe this project as:

- public source-available
- non-commercial
- community research

Do not describe it as:

- MIT licensed
- Apache licensed
- permissive open source
- OSI open source

## Generated Data

The generated `atlas.json` payload does not loosen these terms.

It is a runtime projection of the curated dataset, not a separate commercially reusable feed.
