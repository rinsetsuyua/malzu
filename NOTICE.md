Some public source records cite government advisories and public alerts.

## Citation-Only Sources

Vendor research, academic papers, and public threat-intelligence reports are citation-only unless their licenses explicitly allow broader reuse.

The atlas stores:

- URLs
- metadata
- short curator-written claim summaries

The atlas does not store copied report prose, diagrams, screenshots, tables, logos, IOC dumps, or detection rules.
